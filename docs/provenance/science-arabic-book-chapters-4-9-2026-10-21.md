## Provenance and caveats

- Source: `Science_Ar_prim5_t1.pdf` (scanned, no text layer). PDF page = printed page + 3.
- Every حاول / تدرَّب question and the p. 63 review page are translated. The book prints no answer key; **answers are authored**.
- p. 59 Q1 microscope labelling: letters read from the picture as a = eyepiece, b = adjustment knob, c = mirror, d = stage, e = objective lens. Parent check.
- p. 59 Q3 ordering keyed as b → c → d → a, following the book's own procedure on p. 57.
- p. 71 Q5 and p. 79 Q6 are open-ended; the model answers are suggestions.
- 7-3 comparison table: rows for fish (similar example), camel and frog were filled in by the author; the book leaves them blank for the pupil.
- "Extra" question sets in Chapters 2, 4, 8 and 9 and the whole revision exam are newly written practice, not from the book.
