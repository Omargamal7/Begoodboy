## Parent and source notes

- This is a revision ledger of the supplied Grade 5 assessment, printed/PDF pp4–35. It is not a verified monthly-exam cutoff or an official school answer key.
- All six weekly A/B/C trios are included: Units 1, 2 and 3 each have two trios, for 18 models total.
- Frequency corrections on pp5, 8–9 and 12 often do not determine one unique adverb. These are manual; an adult should accept sensible alternatives rather than impose an invented key.
- Picture-only matching p5 includes all five lines. The picture warmups on pp14 and 30 are also retained as manual source-page tasks.
- The Giving Tree and The Two Frogs complete story texts are not in the assessment. Source-specific story details and morals remain manual. Refer to the Student’s Book or class copy.
- The Nile Lesson 1 descriptions, the named Ancient Egyptian sandstorm p28, Mazen’s uncle’s flood event p27 and some source-specific vocabulary lack independent passage evidence. They are manual and unscored.
- The large-(fish) correction on p34 is ambiguous: fish is already grammatical. No single replacement has been invented.
- Source defects include his my hands p9; give versus gives p14; aren’t milk p15; Thirty (40) word counts pp15–16; all-night TV advice p21; malformed health sentences p22; mixed thunder/lightning p23 and p31; missing The in the heart sentence p34.
- Punctuation and open comprehension are manually checked to allow valid alternatives. The worked answer is an example, not the only accepted wording.
- American spelling and punctuation are accepted. Favorite is the source spelling; do not penalize correct US variants or require memorized wording.
- Writing and projects use checklists. Word counts are practice guidance, not an invented official marking threshold. No model paragraph needs to be memorized.
- One-word gaps from original passages retain the original word banks and complete passage wording. The lesson and model activities are not silently merged or omitted.
