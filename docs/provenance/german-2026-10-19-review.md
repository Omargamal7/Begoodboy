## Confirmed answer-key errors

**October key PDF p2, III Q8:** source row is `du – Ich – sie – neun`. The yellow highlight marks **Ich**. This is erroneous: **neun** is the number; the other three are personal pronouns. Capitalization alone does not make Ich the odd grammatical category here, since other pronouns are capitalized inconsistently elsewhere in this same exercise.

Recommended treatment: show **neun** as the language-correct answer, explicitly flag the disagreement with the school key, and avoid penalizing a learner for noticing the key's highlighted choice. Do not silently describe the incorrect highlighted Ich as the correct answer.

For reference, visually observed key choices for III Q1–9 are: Du, gut, super, Wie, schlecht, ist, Nein, **Ich (disputed)**, Tschüss. The supported category answer for Q8 is neun.

**Booklet PDF p27, Übung 22, age reply:** full-page visual inspection confirms a large pink **9** above the birthday character. The highlighted answer nevertheless reads `Ich bin zehn/elf Jahre alt.` (ten/eleven). The picture and keyed reply therefore conflict. The picture-grounded response is **Ich bin neun Jahre alt.** Keep this task **manual and unscored**, explain the picture/key disagreement, and do not enforce ten or eleven as a sole correct age. An earlier text-only interpretation that there was no fixed age clue was incorrect; the image supplies one.

## Spelling and normalization

Normalize the extraction's Greek-looking `β` to the German `ß` in heißen, heißt, heiße, Grüß and Begrüßen. Preserve umlauts in fünf, zwölf, Tschüss and Grüße where used. Normalize obvious source typos: `Faru` → `Frau`, `schelcht` → `schlecht`, `Auf Widersehen` → `Auf Wiedersehen`. `Tschüs` and `Tschüss` both occur in the material; accept the source's farewell alternatives. Do not treat an extraction artifact as a new German form.

## Recommended grading behavior

Use the school-provided name, numeral or image clue, rather than guessing a child's age/name. Accept the key's explicit alternatives. Keep open personal-response dialogues parent checked or unscored. For image tasks, retain the source image or replace the presentation with a clear textual clue describing the same observation; do not generate an unrelated scene and attach the school's key. Flag the confirmed III Q8 key error, the Übung 22 age-picture/key contradiction, and the ambiguous evening icon for review. The cards themselves teach only the school-observed patterns and vocabulary.
