# Ministry two-book source requirement

For every applicable subject and grade, collect (1) the full Ministry student textbook and (2) the full Performance and Assessment book, matching the school year, term, language and actual school syllabus. Keep school binders, revision sheets and model-answer files as additional sources; they do not close either Ministry-book gap.

Scope: Primary 5 and Primary 6, first term 2026–2027. Science and Mathematics in English; Arabic, Social Studies and Islamic Education in Arabic. Verify ICT's language against school material. English ordinary level must match the assigned syllabus; English advanced level/Wonders remains a separate school source track. Grade 5 German requires its actual assigned course books; do not presume a Ministry pair. Grade 6 has no German course.

## Lesson-pack rule

A chapter pack must map separate lessons to the relevant textbook pages, corresponding Homework Performance sections, and textbook exercises plus assessment models. Preserve original wording and diagrams. Distinguish printed page numbers from PDF indices. Keep school-assigned scope separate from broader available practice. Record missing keys and edition conflicts; do not invent grading keys. Verify both original PDFs before labelling a subject complete.

This inventory establishes the requirement and records gaps; it does not claim that all missing books have been collected.
