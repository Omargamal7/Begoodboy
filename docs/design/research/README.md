## Decisions that can change the HTML

| Decision | What the reports say | What to settle before changing a page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson duration | Gemini proposes 5–15-minute modules. ChatGPT and Claude make pacing an individual decision. The authoring guide already describes the intended sittings. | Keep the current learner setting and pause/resume behavior unless direct feedback supports a change. [Authoring guide](../../authoring.md) |
| Rewards and themes | Gemini asks for a visible crafting-token bar for each completed card or item. Claude suggests a predictable reward and places reward screens between sections. ChatGPT asks whether the theme and reward help this learner. | Decide whether a token bar helps a particular lesson. Keep it outside independent scoring. Record the basis for adopting it. |
| Colour controls | Gemini asks for two or three switchable tints. Claude’s check 17 requires at least one alternative colour theme. ChatGPT asks whether the selected palette is acceptable to the learner and passes contrast requirements. | Check the existing theme control and contrast in each state. Decide whether an additional tint would help. |
| Multiple-choice options | Gemini starts with three options and allows four when the school requires it. Claude also discusses three versus four. ChatGPT calls for confirming school assessment conventions. | Use the actual booklet or school format. Record any question conversion and its answer source. |

## How to review the differences

Pick a rule that would change a visible feature. Record the three reports’ positions, the primary source and the population or standard it covers. Distinguish a measured learner outcome, a general accessibility rule and an individual preference. Then record the decision, the affected part of the HTML and how a reviewer can verify it. The [page structure](../../page-structure.md) and [12-stage workflow](../../html-workflow.md) show where decisions and results belong.

For example, a rule about breaks can be verified by pausing, reloading and resuming at the same place. A proposed fixed session length needs separate evidence. The three checklists cover overlapping topics and their counts should be read separately.
