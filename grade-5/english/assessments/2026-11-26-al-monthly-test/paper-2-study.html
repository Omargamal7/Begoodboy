Fiction Genres & Context Clues

Realistic FictionA made-up story with characters, settings, and problems that could happen in real life (no magic or talking animals).
FolktaleA traditional story passed down over generations. It often has a moral/lesson, clever heroes, or magical elements.
Context CluesHints in the surrounding words or sentences (definitions, examples, synonyms, or contrasts) that unlock an unfamiliar word.
Reading StrategyAlways check: Who is the main character? What is the setting? What problem happens? How does the character solve it? What lesson is learned?

Figurative Language Toolkit

SimileCompares two unlike things using like or as.
"The waves roared like hungry lions."
MetaphorCompares two unlike things directly without like or as.
"The old ship was a sleeping giant."
PersonificationGives human actions or feelings to non-human objects.
"The wooden deck groaned under our boots."
IdiomA special expression whose meaning is different from the literal words.
"Put on a thinking cap" = think carefully.
"Roll up your sleeves" = get ready to work hard.
"Like finding a needle in a haystack" = very hard to find.

Paper II · Narrative Writing Blueprint

5 Steps + Worked Story Model

1. Hook & SettingStart with sound, weather, or action. Name where and when.
2. Characters & GoalIntroduce who is in the story and what they want to do.
3. Problem / ConflictSomething goes wrong (storm, mess, lost item, tough choice).
4. ClimaxThe most exciting or tense moment where the character takes action.
5. Resolution & LessonHow the problem ends and what the character feels or learns.

Worked Narrative Model (Prompt 2 Vocabulary)

Dark clouds gathered above the harbor as the sea was restless against the rocky pier. My cousin and I climbed onto the old fishing boat to find my grandfather's brass compass, though searching the cluttered cabin felt like finding a needle in a haystack.

Beneath our sneakers, the wooden wreck creaked in the gloomy light. I had to hold my breath when I spotted an ancient box resting near a pile of rotting ropes. As I lifted the rusty lid, a piece of wood disintegrated into dust—and inside lay the compass, its needle glowing with a mesmerizing silver shine. We raced back to shore beaming with pride, knowing teamwork had saved a family treasure.
