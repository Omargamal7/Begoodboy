A Day to Remember
Walking down memory lane, I returned to the park where I used to play
with my friends. The sunlight danced on the grass as green trees swayed
slowly in the breeze. Suddenly, the jingly tune of the ice cream truck made
my heart race, and I grinned from ear to ear. Laughter bubbled out of me
like soda fizz as distant sounds of children shouting and bike bells ringing
filled the air. For a moment, the world went quiet, like someone had
pressed pause, and I wished I could stay there forever.
