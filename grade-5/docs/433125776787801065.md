Home Dictation #2

The Seed of Change

The park was filled with chaos because of overflowing trash bins. Maya
was determined to help and put on her thinking cap to find a solution.
She thought outside the box and suggested placing vibrant recycling
bins around the park. Her idea planted the seed of change, and soon,
everyone began keeping the park clean.
