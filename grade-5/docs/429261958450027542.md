English Language Arts

Home Dictation Routine

Home dictation will be completed every two weeks.
1. Read and Practice
A PDF containing the dictation text will be provided. Students should read the
text carefully and practice reading it before the dictation.
2. Listen and Practice
Two audio files will be provided:
o Audio 1: The text is read at a regular speed for listening and reading
practice.
o Audio 2: The text is read at a slower speed and should be used for the
actual home dictation.
3. Write the Dictation
The student should play the slow-speed audio to dictate the text. The student
writes the sentences in the dictation copybook.
4. Correct the Dictation
The parent or adult checks the student's work against the PDF and marks any
incorrectly written words.
5. Rewrite Incorrect Words
Students must rewrite each word they spelled incorrectly to practice the correct
spelling.
6. Bring the Copybook to School
The dictation copybook must be brought to class and shown to the teacher
every following Thursday.
