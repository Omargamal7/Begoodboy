Home Dictation #1

Changing Perspectives

New experiences can change the way we think. They help us see the
bigger picture and open our eyes to new ideas. Meeting people with
different points of view can help us see eye to eye. Sometimes, an
experience can turn our thinking around and help us see things in a new
light.
