Home Dictation #2

The Joy of Helping Others

Being responsible is important because it helps us grow and help others.
Even when a task is time-consuming, we can stay calm and do our best.
When we plan our time, we are less likely to bite off more than we can
chew. Taking on responsibility can be a blessing in disguise and a life-
enhancing experience.
