Correct order: Mouth →Pharynx →Esophagus →Stomach →Small intestine →
Large intestine.
B. What would happen if the heart did not pump blood to the cells?
The cells would not receive the oxygen and nutrients they need, and wastes
would not be carried away. The cells would stop functioning and the body
would die.

Question 5:
Give reasons for the following:
A. The candle flame goes out when it is covered with a glass beaker.
Because the candle uses up the available oxygen inside the beaker. When
there is not enough oxygen to support burning, the flame goes out.
B. When iodine solution is added to the bread, it does not turn blue.
Because the starch in the bread has been broken down by saliva during
digestion, so there is no starch left to turn iodine blue-black.
C. We are advised to chew food well.
Chewing breaks food into smaller pieces and mixes it with saliva, making it
easier to swallow and helping digestion begin.
Question 6:
Complete the following:
Oxygen gas enters through the nose during inhalation, then passes through
the trachea and bronchi until it reaches the lungs, and from there to the
alveoli, which are surrounded by blood capillaries. The blood capillaries carry
oxygen gas to the body parts, while they carry carbon dioxide gas to the
lungs, where it leaves the body during the process of exhalation.
