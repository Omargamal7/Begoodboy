Group (A) – Model Answers
1- Complete the following:
a. The process of breaking down food and changing it into a simpler form
that the body can use is called digestion.
b. The stomach and intestines produce digestive enzymes that chemically
break down food.
2- Put (✓) for the correct statement and (✗) for the incorrect statement:

a. Digestion is the process of changing food into a form that can be easily
absorbed. (✓)
b. Food moves from the mouth to the stomach after chewing. (✓)
3- What is the function of saliva in the digestion of food in the mouth?
Saliva moistens and softens food, making it easier to swallow. It also helps in
breaking down starch into simple sugar.

Groups (B) - Model Answers
1- Complete the following:
a. The liquid secreted in the mouth that contains enzymes that break down
starch is called saliva.
b. The muscular tube through which food moves from the mouth to the
stomach is called the esophagus.
2- Put (✓) for the correct statement and (✗) for the incorrect
statement:
a. The digestion process is the transformation of food to a form easily
absorbed. (✓)
b. Food enters the pharynx through the esophagus. (✗)
3- What happens if undigested food reaches the anus?
Undigested waste is expelled from the body through the anus as feces.

Group (C) – Model answers
1- Put (✓) for the correct statement and (✗) for the incorrect
statement:
a. The color of iodine solution changes to blue-black when it is added to a
test tube containing starch without saliva. (✓)
b. Saliva digests food completely in the mouth. (✗)

2- Compare between: mouth and anus in terms of their functions.
Mouth: takes in and chews food, mixes it with saliva, and begins starch
digestion.
Anus: allows feces to leave the body.
3- What would happen if food reached the mouth without being chewed
by the teeth and mixed with saliva?
Food would not be broken into small pieces or moistened properly. Starch
digestion would not begin effectively in the mouth, making swallowing and
further digestion more difficult.
