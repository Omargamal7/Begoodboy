# Gr. 6- The Gratitude Jar

- **Grade**: grade-6
- **Source**: Announcement (2026-11-04)
- **Pages**: 1

---

Dear Parents,

We are excited to introduce a heartwarming activity called "The Gratitude
Jar!" Inspired by “The Force of G”, a book written by our beloved Yusuf
Ibrahim, a 3rd Secondary student at MLS.

Every day, Grade 6 students will be invited to write one thing they are grateful
for. It could be something simple that made them happy, someone who helped
them, an act of kindness they experienced, or even a difficult situation or
challenge that taught them a valuable lesson. After all, gratitude is also about
finding something positive to appreciate in life's challenges.

Each student must write their name on the paper before placing it in the jar.

A weekly winner will be randomly selected and rewarded with a copy of “The
Force of G,” written by our very own MLS student, Yusuf Ibrahim!

This activity aims to encourage students to appreciate the little things that
make a big difference in our lives, cultivate gratitude, learn from life's
challenges, and develop a positive mindset.

Thank you for your cooperation and support!

Best regards,
