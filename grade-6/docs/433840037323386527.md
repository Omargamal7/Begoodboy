Second: Homework (Homework Task) model answers
1) Complete the following sentences.

a. The burning requires oxygen gas
b. Things keep burning due to the flow of fresh air (oxygen)
c. The gas produced by combustion is carbon dioxide
d. Air is a mixture of gases, such as nitrogen oxygen, and carbon dioxide
e. The percentage of oxygen gas in the air is 21%
2) Give reason for each of the following.

a. The candle flame goes out inside a closed container.
Due to the consumption of oxygen.
b. The candle flame keeps burning when it is placed in an open cup.
Due to the continuous flow of fresh air (oxygen).
3) Think and conclude.

a. Why does the candle does not keep burning for a long time inside a sealed container?
Because there is no fresh air and oxygen inside the container is used up.
b. How can you know that air changes after burning?
Use lime water or gas detector to test for carbon dioxide.
c. What is the importance of oxygen for a candle flame?
It helps in burning.
d. Does moving air help things to burn?
The continuous flow of fresh air allows burning to continue.
4) Compare between:

a. The composition of air before and after burning
Before burning: the air contains nitrogen, oxygen and 0.04% carbon dioxide.
After burning: the oxygen decreases (used up) and the percentage of carbon dioxide
increases.
b. Flame of two candles, one placed in a closed cup and the other in an open cup.
The candle flame in the opened cup: continues burning due to fresh air.
The candle flame in the closed cup: burning stops as oxygen is used up.
