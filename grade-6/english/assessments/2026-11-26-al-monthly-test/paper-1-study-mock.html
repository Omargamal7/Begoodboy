November Test Guide — Paper I

This study page has two clear steps. You can work at your own pace. There is no timer, no sound, and nothing moves until you press a button.

Step 1 — 6 Short Lesson Cards (about 5–8 minutes)
  • Part 1: Nonfiction Genres (Expository Text & Biography) + Context Clues
  • Part 2: Figurative Language (Simile, Metaphor, Personification, Idioms)
  • Part 3: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous
  • Part 4: Stative Verbs (verbs that do not use -ing)
  • Part 5: If Conditionals (Type 0 & Type 1) + Will vs. Be Going To
  • Part 6: Argumentative Writing (4-Paragraph Structure + Worked Model)
Step 2 — 18 Objective Mock Exam Questions (about 10 minutes)
  • Multiple choice and single-idea True/False questions only.
  • After you press Check my answer, you see calm feedback and why.
  • You earn 1 reward block for each completed section or correct group.
