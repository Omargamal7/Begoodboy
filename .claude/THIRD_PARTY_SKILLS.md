## Considered and not pinned

- `pda-reframing` from https://github.com/emory/ASD-AuDHD-PAI-Skills (MIT): it rephrases everyday requests for people with a demand-avoidance (PDA) profile, cites no sources, and its indirect wording conflicts with the design guide's literal-language rule.
- `artifact-design`: the only public copies found were in a repository of leaked system prompts and in a third-party hosting product's skill set. Stage 12 uses the built-in Artifact publishing instead.
- `bloom-taxonomy-quiz-builder-skill` from https://github.com/bobuel/bloom-taxonomy-quiz-builder-skill: it has no licence, so none of its text is copied. Stage 7's checks cover similar ideas in this repository's own words.
